Info

This app applies the "half your age plus seven" rule: the youngest person you may date is age / 2 + 7. Enter both birthdays and it shows the earliest date the pair is allowed to date — green once that date has passed, red while it's still ahead.

The maths simplifies nicely. Both people age at the same rate, so their age gap D (in years) never changes. Writing the older age as younger + D and plugging into younger ≥ older/2 + 7 gives younger ≥ (younger + D)/2 + 7, which reduces all the way down to younger ≥ D + 14. So the pair becomes legal exactly when the younger person turns D + 14 — that's the single moment the app solves for.

The Earliest / Latest buttons set one person's birthday to the oldest / youngest they could be while still legal, given the other person's date. A year is 365.2425 days; the older person counts as born at 12:00am and the younger at 11:59pm, and any partial day rounds up. By default everything is judged against today — untick the box above to test against any other date instead.
